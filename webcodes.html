Contents

Four parts
I

Learn

See how a page is made.

Each Book opens on a real web page, drawn by hand. Break it down and the page lifts apart into its layers in 3D: header, nav, article, footer. Tap any layer to see the tag that makes it.

  • 11 Books and 42 chapters across HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • One idea at a time, about six minutes each
  • Hands-on challenges that check your code as you go
II

Works

Build whole websites from a brief.

The Works are six commissions from the city, each with a brief and goals to meet. Put what you learned together and make something that works from top to bottom.

  • A star atlas, a music box and a riddle gate
  • A district guide, a timeline of the web and a memory game
III

Workshop

Make your own.

Start a project with its own HTML, CSS and JavaScript files and run it instantly in the built-in browser. A scratch pad is always open for quick tries.

  • A code editor that highlights your code and suggests tags
  • A keyboard bar for the symbols you type most
  • Everything runs on your device, even offline
IV

You

Share what you've learned.

Your progress becomes a one-page portfolio website. Pick one of four designs, add your skills and links, and share it as a picture or as a real web page.

  • Four portfolio designs
  • Seals to earn as you finish Books
On iPad

Room to read and build

On iPad the page drawing and the lesson sit side by side, with the shelf of Books below, so you can follow along with everything in view.
