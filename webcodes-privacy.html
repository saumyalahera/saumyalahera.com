Privacy policy

Privacy

Web Codes does not collect any data. Your progress and projects stay on your device.

Web Codes for iPhone & iPad · Last updated 10 November 2026

IWhat Web Codes collects

Nothing. Web Codes has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't send your code, progress or anything else you make to us or to anyone else. We don't receive, sell or share any personal information.

IIWhat stays on your device

To work, Web Codes saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad. None of it leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

  • Your progress: the Books and chapters you've finished and the seals you've earned.
  • Your projects: the HTML, CSS and JavaScript you write in the Workshop and the Works.
  • Your portfolio: the name, skills and links you add, and the design you pick.

You can change or delete any of this in the app. Deleting Web Codes removes all of it. If your device backs up to iCloud, this data is included in that backup, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

IIIRunning your code

When you run a project, Web Codes shows it in a web view on your device. Your code is not uploaded anywhere. If your own code loads something from the internet, such as an image, a font or a script, your device fetches it directly from that site, just as a browser would. The portfolio page the app makes links to Google Fonts for its typefaces, so a person who opens it may load those fonts from Google.

IVSharing

When you share your portfolio as a picture or a web page, Web Codes hands it to iOS. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you share it with.

VChildren

Web Codes is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

VIChanges

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
