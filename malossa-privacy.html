Privacy

Malossa does not collect any data. Everything you make stays on your Mac.

Malossa for macOS · Last updated 17 October 2026

What Malossa collects

Nothing. Malossa has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn't send anything you write or make over the internet. We don't receive, sell or share any personal information.

What stays on your Mac

To work, Malossa saves a few things locally. None of it leaves your Mac unless you choose to move it.

  • Your projects, saved in the folders you choose.
  • Your developer profile: the name, role, bio, links and photo you add for your business card.
  • Settings and history: theme, code font, recent and favourite projects, and the days you worked, used for the activity on your card.

You can change or delete any of this in the app, or by deleting the files from your Mac. Removing Malossa removes its settings.

Sharing

When you save or share an image, such as your business card, Malossa hands it to macOS. Where it goes from there is up to you and the app you share it with.

Children

Malossa collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
