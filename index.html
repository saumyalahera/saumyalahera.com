Research focus

I’m interested in how carefully exposing architectural structure (like tile locality) can unlock safe, automatic optimization — and make interactive systems feel more predictable and “alive.”

Tile‑Aware Pass Fusion

Tile‑based GPUs execute fragments in on‑chip tile memory, yet many pipelines force intermediate writes to device memory. I explore restructuring passes so data stays tile‑local, reducing bandwidth, latency, and energy.

Outcome: improved frame stability + efficiency without changing visuals.

TileLang

TileLang makes locality explicit through residency semantics. A compiler can then safely fuse passes and remove transient resources, producing efficient Metal pipelines while keeping code expressive for rapid iteration.

Outcome: architecture‑aware optimization that is repeatable and less error‑prone.

The World's Most Coveted Teas

These teas command extraordinary prices for their scarcity, terroir, or the precision required to produce them. A few hundred grams can cost more than a fine bottle of wine.

Da Hong Pao
Rock OolongWuyi, China

From six original mother bushes on a cliffside. Cuttings now exist, but true Da Hong Pao is essentially priceless — rich, roasted, mineral.

Gyokuro Asahi
Shade-Grown GreenUji, Japan

Shaded for up to 35 days, harvested by hand. The extended shade period pushes L-theanine to extreme levels — intensely umami, almost broth-like.

Silver Needle Tribute
White TeaFujian, China

Only the very first buds of spring, harvested within a 2-week window. Each bud covered in fine white hairs. Honey, melon, wildflower in the cup.

Aged Pu-erh Cake
FermentedYunnan, China

A 30-year-old raw pu-erh develops complexity rivalling aged wine — notes of dark forest, dried fruit, earth. Cakes from famous estates fetch thousands.
