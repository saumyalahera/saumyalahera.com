Triangle from sector + projection

What this is showing:
For each pixel p = (x, y), compute its angle a.
Pick the nearest triangle direction: 0°, 120°, or 240°.
Then compute d = dot(p, dir).
Pixels with d ≈ C become the bright edge band.
Inside a single sector, dot(p, dir) = C is a line. Three sectors give three lines. Those three lines form the triangle.
The red point is one sample pixel. The green segment is its projection onto the selected direction.
