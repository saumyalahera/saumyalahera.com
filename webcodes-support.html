IQuestions

Is Web Codes free?

Yes. There are no ads and no account to make.

Do I need to know anything about code to start?

No. The first Book starts from zero and explains what a web page is before you write any code. Each chapter covers one idea and takes about six minutes.

How do I open a Book?

In Learn, tap a spine on the shelf to open that Book, then tap a chapter to start. The Book you're on is shown open on the shelf, and "Up next" takes you straight to your next chapter.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The lessons, Works and Workshop all run on your device. Only code that you write yourself to load something from the internet, such as an image link, needs a connection.

My project shows a blank page. What should I check?

Check that your HTML has content inside <body>, that every tag you open is closed, and that the file names you link to match exactly, including capital letters. If it still looks wrong, try the same code in the scratch pad to narrow it down.

How do I share my portfolio?

Open the You tab, pick one of the four designs, and add your skills and links. Then use the share button to send it as a picture or as a web page file.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone or iPad?

Your progress and projects are saved on your device. They come along when you restore a new device from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one.

How do I start over?

Deleting and reinstalling Web Codes clears all progress, projects and your portfolio. This can't be undone, so share or copy anything you want to keep first.

What data does Web Codes collect?

None. See the privacy policy for the details.

IIReporting a problem

If something isn't working, send an email with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot helps a lot.
